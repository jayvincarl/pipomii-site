# Pipomii Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 13, 2026_

Pipomii is built to keep your information with you: on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud Drive for backups.

## What Pipomii stores
Everything you enter or record: your activities, timer sessions, habit check-ins, counts, goals, notes and settings. It is stored on your device. Pipomii has no accounts and no servers of its own, and it never sends this information to us or to anyone else.

## What Pipomii does not do
- It does not collect your personal data.
- It does not track you or use analytics or advertising tools.
- It does not share or sell any information.

## Backups and exports
Pipomii keeps an automatic daily backup of your data. When iCloud Drive is on, it is saved to your own iCloud Drive (in a Pipomii folder), which Apple stores under your Apple Account; otherwise it stays on your device. We, the makers of Pipomii, cannot see or reach it. You can turn automatic backup off in Settings › Backup and restore, and delete the files at any time in the Files app.

If you choose to save a backup file or export your data yourself, the file is created on your device and shared only where you send it. That is your choice and under your control.

## Notifications
Reminders you set are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server.

## Children
Pipomii does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
