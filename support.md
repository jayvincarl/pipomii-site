# Pipomii Support

## Contact
Email: pipomii.support@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. Settings → Send feedback fills these in for you.

## Common questions
**Where is my data?** On your iPhone. Pipomii also keeps an automatic daily backup in your own iCloud Drive (or on your iPhone when iCloud Drive is off). There are no accounts and no servers.

**How do I move to a new phone?** Sign in to the same iCloud account and install Pipomii. It finds your latest backup and offers to restore it. You can also restore any backup file from Settings → Backup and restore.

**I restored or deleted something by mistake.** Settings → Backup and restore → Undo last restore. It keeps one copy of what was there. Older automatic backups are kept too: daily copies for a week and weekly copies for a month.

**My reminders aren't arriving.** Check that notifications are on for Pipomii in iOS Settings → Notifications. A reminder is skipped on days the activity is already done, and on rest days.

**A widget isn't updating.** Open Pipomii once, then check the widget again. If it still looks old, remove it and add it again.
